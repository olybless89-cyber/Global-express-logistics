Who We Are

DHL EXPRESS LOGISTICS is a courier and logistics service built around the needs of our customers. We handle domestic and international shipments with care, from registration and pickup through warehousing, transport and customs clearance to final delivery. We offer premium, express and economy parcel options at reasonable rates, and every shipment can be followed online with its tracking number. We value one-to-one relationships with our customers and aim to keep every delivery simple, transparent and on time.
